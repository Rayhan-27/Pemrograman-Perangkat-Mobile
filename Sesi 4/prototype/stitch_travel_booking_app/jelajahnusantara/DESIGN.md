## Brand & Style

The brand personality captures the spirit of archipelago adventure: vibrant, hospitable, breathtaking, and effortlessly dependable. It balances the pristine natural beauty of Indonesia's rainforests and volcanic peaks with the warmth of tropical golden hours. 

The aesthetic is modern, fluid, and tactile—combining crisp editorial layouts with high-touch travel cards, immersive imagery, and warm micro-interactions. The target audience includes domestic and international explorers seeking off-the-beaten-path expeditions, cultural retreats, and island gateways across the Indonesian archipelago. The interface evokes wanderlust, serene confidence, and modern tropical vitality.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the natural geography of the archipelago:
- **Primary (`#0F766E` - Deep Rainforest Emerald)**: Commands key navigation bars, primary booking actions, and high-emphasis anchors. Conveys depth, stewardship, and tranquility.
- **Secondary (`#14B8A6` - Island Teal)**: Drives interactive active states, progress indicators, floating pill accents, and secondary toggles. Evokes tropical waters and coral reefs.
- **Tertiary (`#F97316` - Sunset Coral / Amber)**: Deployed intentionally for time-sensitive highlights, booking confirmations, promotional tags, flash badges, and dynamic ratings.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Volcanic Slate)**: Serves as the authoritative text and structural baseline, providing crisp legibility against bright tropical photo assets.
- **Canvas (`#F8FAFC` to `#FFFFFF`)**: Ensures high contrast, generous optical breathing room, and a sunlit, clean atmosphere.

## Typography

The design system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across headlines, body, and micro-labels. Originally crafted with Indonesian design heritage in mind, its contemporary geometry and rounded aperatures provide welcoming warmth, extreme legibility on high-density mobile screens, and an optimistic rhythm.

- **Display & Headlines**: Tightly kerned with negative letter-spacing to form cohesive, impactful titles over landscape photography and hero headers.
- **Body Text**: Tuned with generous line-height to make long travel itineraries, cultural descriptions, and transport guides comfortable to read in full outdoor sunlight.
- **Labels & Badges**: Set with deliberate medium-to-bold weights and slight positive tracking to ensure instant scannability on badges, ratings, and bottom navigation labels.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid architecture built to adapt seamlessly from one-handed mobile navigation to wide tablet/desktop discovery portals:

- **Mobile (<640px)**: 4-column layout with a standard `1rem` (16px) margin and `1rem` gutter. Content stretches to card boundaries with horizontal-scroll carousels breaking through outer bounds to bleed edge-to-edge for immersive exploration.
- **Tablet (640px–1024px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Ideal for dual-pane travel planners and interactive maps side-by-side with destination cards.
- **Desktop (>1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at a maximum container width of `1280px` with `3rem` margins to preserve optimal viewing proportions.

Vertical rhythm follows a strict 8pt base grid (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) to maintain consistent breathing room between content sections, touch targets, and metadata badges.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through ambient, natural light projection reminiscent of bright island sun, relying on soft, multi-layered shadows tinted with the primary hue rather than dead grays:

- **Surface Floor (`Level 0`)**: `#F8FAFC` base canvas providing a crisp, glare-free backdrop.
- **Resting Cards (`Level 1`)**: `#FFFFFF` background with `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.03)`. Subtle, crisp, and tactile.
- **Floating Controls & Modals (`Level 2`)**: Interactive bottom sheets, sticky search pills, and floating action buttons use `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.04)`.
- **Active / Dragged Elements (`Level 3`)**: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.06)`.
- **Glass Overlays**: Destination hero cards and media carousels feature subtle frosted scrims (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) for weather chips, price badges, and quick-action icons.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a friendly, ergonomic rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2` base):
- **Base Components (0.5rem / 8px)**: Input fields, checkboxes, list tiles, and secondary buttons.
- **Cards & Sheets (1rem / 16px - `rounded-lg`)**: Destination previews, ticket holders, modal sheets, and gallery frames.
- **Pills & Chips (9999px / Full pill)**: Category filters, status indicators, primary CTA buttons, floating bottom tabs, and price/rating badges.

This curvature mimics the organic shapes of river stones, volcanic contours, and sea shells, removing harsh technical corners to sustain an inviting travel mood.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Full pill shape, solid `#0F766E` background with white `label-lg` typography. Minimum 48px touch target. Hover/active states blend toward `#14B8A6`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pill shape with 1.5px border in `#14B8A6` and text in `#0F766E`.
- **Sunset Accent CTA**: Solid `#F97316` fill reserved for primary conversion triggers (e.g., "Book Expedition Now", "Claim Promo").

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Chips**: Pill-shaped horizontal selector with `#FFFFFF` background, `#E2E8F0` border, and `#0F172A` text. Active state transitions to `#0F766E` background with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Floating Metadata Badges**: Frosted glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` + blur) or solid `#F97316` for live deals, star ratings (accompanied by gold star icon), and eco-certified trip markers.

### Cards
- **Destination & Tour Cards**: 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg`), elevated with Level 1 ambient shadow. Bleed image hero with a 16:9 or 4:5 portrait ratio, overlaid with frosted-glass badge (top-right) and sunset coral price tag (bottom-right). Generous 16px internal padding for title, island location, and duration meta.

### Form Inputs & Search Bar
- **Global Search Anchor**: Floating pill input with search icon, placeholder "Cari pantai, gunung, atau kuliner...", and an emerald quick-filter action icon on the right edge.
- **Text Inputs**: 8px corner radius, `#FFFFFF` fill with 1.5px `#E2E8F0` border, shifting to `#14B8A6` focus ring with 3px subtle outer glow (`rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes**: 8px rounded corners with `#0F766E` check fill and white checkmark icon.
- **Radio Buttons**: Smooth circular boundary with an internal teal dot transition for package selections and travel class picks.

### Lists & Itinerary Tiles
- **Timeline Lists**: Connected vertical trail lines in `#14B8A6` featuring numbered or icon-based emerald nodes representing travel days, rest stops, and summit points. Clean separation using `#F1F5F9` divider lines without visual clutter.

### Travel-Specific Components
- **Island Pass / Ticket Card**: Dual-section card separated by a dashed perforation line and semicircular side cutouts, incorporating QR codes, boarding times, and ferry/flight identifiers.
- **Sticky Bottom Booking Bar**: Elevated floating card anchored above mobile navigation featuring real-time price summary on the left and a prominent `#F97316` "Pesan Sekarang" button on the right.
